# CITA

**Legal Services & Project Marketplace**

> Portfolio case study based on professional project experience. This is a descriptive case study, not a public copy of the product.

## Overview

CITA is a legal bidding portal connecting verified attorneys with clients through project proposals, document exchange, milestone-based payments, and dispute workflows.

## My contribution

**Role:** Full-Stack Developer

- Built features for attorney-client bidding and proposal workflows.
- Worked on encrypted document exchange.
- Implemented milestone-based escrow payment workflows.
- Developed contract negotiation tracking and dispute-management features.

## Technology

Angular · Node.js · Express

## Product capabilities

- Attorney and client workflows
- Project bidding and proposals
- Document exchange
- Milestone and payment tracking
- Contract negotiation and dispute management

## Engineering focus

The work involved coordinating multi-step business workflows and integrating frontend experiences with backend services.
