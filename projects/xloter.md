# Xloter

**Real-Time Sports Ground Booking System**

> Portfolio case study based on professional project experience. The preview is a conceptual design, not a screenshot of the live product.

## Overview

Xloter is a sports venue discovery and booking platform that enables users to find facilities and reserve available game slots, with analytics features for facility owners.

## My contribution

**Role:** Full-Stack & Mobile Engineer

- Contributed to the venue discovery and booking experience.
- Implemented real-time calendar synchronization.
- Developed automated slot-locking mechanisms to prevent double bookings.
- Worked on facility-owner analytics portals.

## Technology

Next.js · React Native · Node.js

## Product capabilities

- Sports venue discovery
- Game-slot reservations
- Calendar synchronization
- Slot locking to prevent booking conflicts
- Facility-owner analytics

## Engineering focus

The key engineering challenge was keeping booking availability synchronized and preventing conflicting reservations during concurrent booking activity.
