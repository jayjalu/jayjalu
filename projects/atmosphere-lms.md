# Atmosphere LMS

**Enterprise Corporate & Industrial Learning Management System**

> Portfolio case study based on professional project experience. The visual preview is conceptual and is not a production screenshot.

## Overview

Atmosphere LMS is an industrial and corporate learning platform supporting course creation, learner progress, video-based training, and assessment reporting.

## My contribution

**Role:** Full-Stack Developer

- Delivered course-creation workflows and learning platform features.
- Worked on SCORM-compliant video-tracking modules.
- Implemented automated progress and assessment reports.
- Contributed to scalable, Docker-containerized backend services supporting concurrent corporate sessions.

## Technology

Angular · Node.js · Docker

## Product capabilities

- Course creation and management
- SCORM-compliant video tracking
- Learner progress monitoring
- Assessment and reporting workflows
- Containerized backend services

## Engineering focus

The project required coordinated learning workflows, reliable progress tracking, and backend services designed to support concurrent training sessions.
